About Us

LinguaLink is a student-led initiative breaking down language and cultural barriers through connection, education, and community. We bring students together across languages and cultures to create more inclusive schools and communities where everyone has the opportunity to be heard, understood, and belong.

Our Vision

We believe language should never be a barrier to belonging.

Our goal is to build inclusive communities where every student feels welcomed, understood, and empowered to thrive—regardless of the language they speak or where they come from.

By connecting students across cultures, we're creating a future where communication brings people together instead of keeping them apart.
